Trust check before an agent pays

How Routex402 fits into an agent execution flow: the agent checks the seller's on-chain reputation, a policy decides whether to proceed, and the payment is signed by the buyer, which is what moves the score.

Read this first. This is an illustration of an integration, not a finished one. It is not a product of Aomi or any other platform, and no partnership is implied. The reputation lookup and policy check below use live Routex402 data. The simulation step is mocked. The final step makes a real devnet payment to the demo seller.
